Release Version 1.0

It's been cooking for a while. Now, with feature parity to the legacy tools and a ton of new features, we're officially calling it a 1.0.

TypeRoof is built for testing and proofing variable fonts—especially those with large design spaces, like parametric fonts. Branching out from its original mission, it now includes extensible toolsets for building parametric layouts, creating complex typographic animations, and offering a foundation for a complete, general-purpose typography platform.

#What’s in the box?

The Shell App takes over as the primary application; the Legacy App remains available.

With the new Forum we look forward to building a community around our idea of parametric typography and among users of the infrastructure that’s been created.

As far as the Shell App is concerned, new features are too abundant to be called out here; instead, we are going to focus on improving the Documentation. However, here are a few highlights:

  • Feature parity with the legacy app.
  • A new architecture with the potential to grow into many use cases, along with a concept for how to use the TypeRoof infrastructure to do so.
  • A complete pipeline for parametric typography and layout, down to customizable justification and up to WYSIWYG editing based on ProseMirror.
  • General purpose animation capabilities that go far beyond the requirements of Video Proof.

Moving forward, we will be using Semantic Versioning (SemVer) and operating on a rolling release basis to deliver continuous improvements.

#History

TypeRoof is the amalgamation of Variable Type Tools—minisites demonstrating various aspects of variable fonts—and Video Proof—experimental tools for creating animated proofs for variable fonts. The former started in 2017 and the latter in 2019. David Berlow of Font Bureau and David Crossland of Google provided direction and vision for the project from its beginnings, with Chris Lewis serving as the initial developer of both tools for Type Network.

Under the banner of Font Bureau, Lasse Fister and his company Strong Type Systems took over maintenance in 2022, initially migrating the tools from PHP to a pure client-side JavaScript implementation. In late 2022, the project was renamed TypeRoof. The merger of the Type Tools and Video Proof apps was retained as the “Legacy” app, while work began on the new architecture—the “Metamodel”—and the “Shell” app.

Version 1.0 establishes feature parity with the Legacy app while introducing numerous new features. It sets exciting new directions for general-purpose animations using variable fonts and lays the foundation for parametric typesetting. Eduardo Omine, who joined the team in August 2025, played a crucial role in the push toward this newly released version.
