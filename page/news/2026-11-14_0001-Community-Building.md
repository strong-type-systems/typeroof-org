TypeRoof is meant to grow with its users and contributors. That is
why we are inviting everyone interested in type tooling, typography,
and free software to help shape how the project evolves.

With the [Community Protocol (Work In Progress)](https://app.typeroof.org/docs/community/community-protocol/)
we propose a new concept for structuring contributions and collaboration,
built around three phases:

* **Inception** — an open playground where anyone can share ideas,
  insights, and rough prototypes as Proposals.
* **Incubation** — Proposals are developed into production-ready
  features through mentored collaboration between Builders and
  Maintainers.
* **Distribution** — autonomous distributions curate and maintain
  their own flavor of TypeRoof, tailored to their users' needs.

The full protocol and the thinking behind it are published on the
site, and both have their own discussion threads in the new forum:

* [The Community Protocol](https://app.typeroof.org/docs/community/community-protocol/)
* [Protocol discussion thread](https://forum.typeroof.org/t/community-protocol/11)
* [From Code Abundance to Human Agency — the thinking behind the
  protocol](https://app.typeroof.org/docs/community/community-protocol-thinking/)
* [Essay discussion thread](https://forum.typeroof.org/t/from-code-abundance-to-human-agency/12)
