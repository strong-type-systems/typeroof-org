TypeRoof is built for testing and proofing variable fonts—especially those
with large design spaces, like parametric fonts. Branching out from its
original mission, it now includes growing toolsets for building parametric
layouts and creating complex typographic animations, evolving into a complete,
general-purpose typography platform.

## What's in the box?

The [Shell App](https://app.typeroof.org/shell) takes over as the primary
application; the [Legacy App](https://app.typeroof.org/legacy) remains available.

With the new [Forum](https://forum.typeroof.org) we look forward to building
a community around our idea of parametric typography and among users of the
infrastructure that's been created.

As far as the [Shell App](https://app.typeroof.org/shell) is concerned, new
features are too abundant to be called out here; instead, we are going to
focus on improving the [Documentation](https://app.typeroof.org/docs).
However, here are a few highlights:

* Feature parity with the legacy app.
* A new architecture with the potential to grow into many use cases, along
  with a concept for how to use the TypeRoof infrastructure to do so.
* A complete pipeline for parametric typography and layout, down to
  customizable justification and up to WYSIWYG editing based on
  [ProseMirror](https://prosemirror.net/).
* General purpose animation capabilities that go far beyond the requirements
  of Video Proof.

Moving forward, we will be using Semantic Versioning (SemVer) and operating
on a rolling release basis to deliver continuous improvements.

## History

TypeRoof is the amalgamation of Variable Type Tools—*minisites demonstrating
various aspects of variable fonts*—and Video Proof—*experimental tools
for creating animated proofs for variable fonts*. The former started in
2017 and the latter in 2019. David Berlow of Font Bureau and David Crossland
of Google provided direction and vision for the project from its beginnings,
with Chris Lewis serving as the initial developer of both tools for
[Type Network](https://typenetwork.com/).

Under the banner of Font Bureau, Lasse Fister and his company
[Strong Type Systems](https://strong-type.systems) took over maintenance
in 2022, initially migrating the tools from PHP to a pure client-side
JavaScript implementation. In late 2022, the project was renamed TypeRoof.
The merger of the Type Tools and Video Proof apps was retained as the
"Legacy" app, while work began on the new architecture—the "Metamodel"—and
the "Shell" app.

Version 1.0 establishes feature parity with the Legacy app while introducing
numerous new features. It sets exciting new directions for general-purpose
animations using variable fonts and lays the foundation for parametric
typesetting. Eduardo Omine, who joined the team in August 2025, played a
crucial role in the push toward this newly released version.
